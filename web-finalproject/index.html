ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding TOD Potential

This WebGIS visualizes accessibility around public transport hubs in Yogyakarta using surrounding facilities and buffer-based analysis.

01

Transport Hubs

Railway stations, bus stops, and terminals are identified as the main transport nodes.

02

Facility Accessibility

Education, healthcare, and commercial facilities are analysed within 400 m and 800 m catchment areas.

03

TOD Potential

Accessibility and facility diversity are combined into a TOD score ranging from 0 to 9.
